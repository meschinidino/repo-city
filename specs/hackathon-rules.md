# Hackathon Rules — Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon

Source: [Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon on Cerebral Valley](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon), fetched 2026-10-26, plus the kickoff slides provided by the participant (2026-10-26). Where a source is a slide, it says so.

## Organizer-posted rules (Discord, Ivan, 2026-10-25 19:50)

Posted by the organizers in the event Discord. "Read these before you build. Breaking them can disqualify your project."

- **New work only.** Everything presented has to be built during the hackathon, Oct 26 to Nov 4.
- **Identify your contributions.** The demo must show only features, code, and functionality the team built during the event. Judges need to be able to tell what is new. If they cannot, the project is disqualified.
- **Public repo.** The submitted repository must be public.
- **Teams.** Max 4 people, solo allowed. Pick one track (Indie Developer or Startup), enter the project in that track only. One person submits for the team.
- **Demo video.** 3 minutes, required on the submission. One of the main things judges score.
- **Banned projects.** Disqualified if they break legal, ethical, or platform policies, or use code, data, or assets you do not have the rights to. Also avoid generic projects: AI medical or health advice, basic RAG apps, simple image analyzers, "AI for education" chatbots, job application screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analyzers, sports analyzers or coaches.
- **Theme.** The project must fit one of the two problem statements: Build for Your World, or Push the Frontier (use Muse Spark, Glimmer, SAM 3, or other Meta models on hard problems in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, or science).
- **Submit** by Wed Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET at https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit — public repo, working demo video link, every team member added.
- **Also in the waiver:** participants must be 18+ and of legal age where they live; Meta or CV staff, contractors, government-affiliated people, and their households cannot participate.

Note the difference from the kickoff slides: this Discord list does not repeat "dashboards as the main feature" in its generic list (the slides do). Treat both lists as active; avoiding dashboards costs nothing.

**Consequence for assets:** nothing here requires Muse-generated assets. The disqualification line is rights: code, data, or assets you do not have the rights to. Provenance logging (`assets/PROVENANCE.md`, planned) is the compliance mechanism.

## Event facts (event page)

- Remote, Oct 26 at 12:00 PM to Nov 4 at 9:00 PM EST. Ten days, $1M in prizes, virtual, worldwide, free if selected.
- Build with: Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, SAM 3.1 through Meta Model API, Meta open-weights models, Muse Code coding agent.
- Inference partners: Baseten ($100 credits, 2M TPM, select participants), Modal ($100 credits, select participants), Fireworks ($25 credits, select participants).
- Tracks (every project enters one):
  - **Indie developer track:** individual builders and students. Judged on creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works.
  - **Startup track:** teams and founders building a real product. Judged on technical execution and whether it holds up in production.
- Themes: **Build for Your World** (solve a problem you know firsthand) or **Push the Frontier** (new capability in software, robotics, science, physical systems).
- Prize split (secondary reporting of the waiver/published rules, verify against Official Rules): Startup track $550K ($250K/$150K/$75K/$50K/$25K), Indie track $400K ($150K/$100K/$75K/$50K/$25K), remainder bonus pot.

## Eligibility (event page)

- Open worldwide except where local law prohibits participation or receipt of an award. Void where prohibited.
- Not eligible: anyone under US/EU/UN/UK trade sanctions or export controls, or acting for someone who is; anyone organized or located in Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Cuba, North Korea, or Iran.
- Not eligible: employees, contractors, and interns of Meta and Cerebral Valley, government-affiliated individuals, their immediate family, and people in the same household.
- Participation limited to 20,000 selected applicants. Duplicate applications, false identity, or spam disqualify.

## Application review (event page, for the record)

Two equally weighted criteria: project idea clarity/feasibility/theme fit, and technical skill shown in a described AI project. Rolling review.

## Submission requirements (kickoff slides)

Submit four things by **Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET**:

1. Submission on the CV platform, with every teammate listed: https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit
2. A **3-minute demo video** showing only what the team built during the event. Judges score from it.
3. A **public GitHub repo**. Check it opens in a logged-out browser before submitting.
4. Before the deadline. Do not wait for the last hour.

## Judging rubric (kickoff slides)

| Criterion | Weight | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | 30% | Real-world potential, who benefits, fit with a problem statement |
| Use of Meta AI tooling | 25% | Creative use beyond a basic integration |
| Technical depth | 20% | Sound, refined engineering with real craft |
| Demo | 25% | A working demo that holds up, and a demo video worth watching |

Same rubric in Stage 2 and the final round. Scores run 0 to 10 per criterion.

## Bonus prizes (kickoff slides), $50K total

- **$20K Most Viral Project:** most engagement on X after the event. Post demo on X and submit the link. Most likes, reposts, and comments by Nov 8, 11:59 PM ET wins.
- **$10K Best Build on Meta Open Weights:** best project on Muse Glimmer or Muse Spark Open, on your own hardware or partner inference. Flagged in round one. The infra partner verifies it runs on an eligible open-weight model.
- **$10K Community Vote:** most votes in the public gallery. Winner announced Nov 16. One vote per verified account.
- **$5K Best Agentic Use-Case:** strongest use of AI agents to complete tasks, make decisions, or run multi-step workflows. Judges score autonomy, effectiveness, practical usefulness.
- **$5K Best Creative Build:** most original creative project built with Muse Image or Muse Wonder. Flagged in round one. Scored on originality, execution, and use of the model.
