Product: Repo City, a game-like app where a code repository is shown as an archipelago and AI coding agents are sent on quests. Concept art for a 3-minute hackathon demo video. 16:9 game screenshot, not a website, not a dashboard.

View: CLOSE-UP of one island, the "payments" module, isometric camera zoomed in so the island fills most of the frame with sea around it. On the island: a small keep with a flag (entry point); three wooden piers reaching into open water, one per API route, each with a small lantern (exposed endpoints); two round silos beside a warehouse (database access); a village of eight simple cottages (ordinary files); one cottage overgrown with ivy, roof caved in, unlit, with no path leading to it (dead code). A small green pennant flies over two buildings and a red pennant over one (test results). A small ship with a colored sail (the agent) is moored at a pier. A tiny figure stands at the door of the warehouse, and the warehouse and one cottage have warm lit windows (the agent is editing those files right now). A thin bright highlighted lane leaves the island toward the edge of the frame, partially built (a new connection in progress).

Above the island, one small floating plain-English caption near the ship: "Muse is adding friend groups: 3 files changed". One slim command bar along the bottom edge with three agent tokens (portrait chip, name, model tag "Claude" or "Muse", one-line quest). No top bar, no sidebar, no chat box, no panels.

Art style: smooth stylized low-poly, like Dorfromantik and Monument Valley 2. Soft matte materials, gentle global light from the upper left, soft ambient occlusion, short soft shadows, a calm pale sea in desaturated teal-gray with subtle shoreline foam. Muted palette with one warm accent for lit windows and the sail. Clean silhouettes, low detail, every building type distinct by shape. Not photorealistic, not busy, no neon glow, no purple gradient, no hard-hat workers, no smoke.
