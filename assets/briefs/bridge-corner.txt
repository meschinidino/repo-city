Bridge corner piece — Repo City sprite pipeline brief

Goal: a corner deck piece that joins two straight bridge decks at a
sharp bend, so bent bridges turn on one clean corner instead of two
overlapping straight tiles (today the joint shows crossing planks,
doubled posts and a water gap at the outer edge).

References attached: the straight deck tile
(assets/sprites/bridge-a.png) for wood, posts and rope style, and a
close-up screenshot of a bent bridge today showing the fault.

Match the reference art: stylized low-poly game art, warm soft light
from the upper left, matte wood, warm brown planks with visible seams
and nail dots, square posts, rope rails.

Make: ONE corner piece in screen space, a V (chevron) band opening to
the RIGHT (east). The outer vertex points LEFT; one arm runs up-right
at about 26 degrees above horizontal, the other runs down-right
mirrored. Interior angle at the vertex about 53 degrees. Deck band
width matches the reference tile's deck width at the same scale. Each
arm is about 1.2 deck-widths long from the vertex and ends in a clean
straight plank cut across the arm (no posts at the cut ends: the
straight decks supply those).

Construction: planks run across each arm (perpendicular to the arm
axis) and fan around the vertex, with no gaps. A stout square corner
post stands at the outer vertex point, with rope rails running from it
along the outer edge of each arm to the cut ends. The inner edges
carry matching rails meeting at a small inner post just back from the
inner point. No water, no ground, no shadow outside the piece, no
text, no people.

Background: solid magenta #FF00FF everywhere outside the piece,
including the V opening between the arms. Full posts and rails inside
the frame; nothing cropped at the frame edges except the two arm ends.
One corner piece only, vertex near left-center, arms reaching the
right edge top and bottom, filling most of the frame.
