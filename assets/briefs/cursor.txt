Mouse cursor — Repo City sprite pipeline brief

Goal: one small custom mouse cursor the page uses (CSS cursor), so
the pointer stays easy to see over sea, grass, and bridges.

Make: a single chunky golden-brass pointer, harbor style: a short
arrow with a small ship-anchor notch in its tail. Thick dark outline,
bright warm gold fill, one small white highlight. Flat game icon
style, no gradients that wash out at small size, readable at 32 px.

Background: solid magenta #FF00FF, no shadow, no text, no other
objects. Cursor points to the upper-left corner of the frame; tip
close to that corner, whole cursor inside the frame.
