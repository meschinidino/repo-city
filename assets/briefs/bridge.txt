Bridge deck texture — Repo City sprite pipeline brief

Goal: a tileable wooden bridge deck texture the renderer uses as the
fill of the code-drawn bridge polygon, so close-up bridges read as
built wood, not a flat brown polygon.

Reference attached: a current close-up screenshot of the map. Match
its art: stylized low-poly game art, warm soft light from the upper
left, matte wood, colors close to the buildings sheet
(assets/sprites/buildings.png).

Make: one straight bridge deck segment seen from the same isometric
angle as the map (diamond projection, long axis running lower-left
to upper-right). Wooden planks across the deck, two side rails with
posts, rope detail under the rails, warm brown wood with visible
plank seams. The deck must tile along its long axis: left and right
ends match so segments can repeat.

Background: solid magenta #FF00FF everywhere outside the deck, no
shadow on the background, no text, no people, no water.
One segment only, centered, filling most of the frame.
