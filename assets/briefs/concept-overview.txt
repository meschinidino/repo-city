Product: Repo City, a game-like app where a code repository is shown as an archipelago and AI coding agents are sent on quests. Concept art for a 3-minute hackathon demo video. 16:9 game screenshot, not a website, not a dashboard.

View: OVERVIEW of the whole map, isometric camera, high and far. Five to seven small islands floating on a calm sea (one island per code module), with generous open water between them so each island reads as a separate place at a glance. Each island has one dominant tint and a few clear landmark buildings, not dense detail. Islands are named with small clean horizontal labels on the water beside them: "web", "api", "auth", "payments", "shared", "tests". Thin bridges or dashed shipping lanes connect some islands (these are code imports). One lane is highlighted as a bright band being built between "api" and "payments". At the bottom-left edge of the map is the player's home harbor with two small docked ships (idle agents). One small ship with a colored sail is moored at the "payments" island, and two buildings on that island have warm lit windows (the agent is working there). One island far off is newly rising from the sea with a soft splash ring (a new module).

Landmarks per island: a keep or small castle (entry point), piers reaching into open water (exposed API endpoints, more piers on "api"), round silos by a warehouse (database), a tall lighthouse on "shared" (a utility everything depends on), clusters of simple cottages (ordinary files), shipping containers on one quay (vendored code), and one dark, overgrown, unlit shack with no path on "web" (dead code). Small green or red pennants fly over a few buildings (test results).

Interface: the only UI is one slim command bar along the bottom edge with three agent tokens, each a small round portrait chip with a name, a model tag ("Claude" or "Muse"), and a one-line quest such as "Add friend groups". No top bar, no sidebar, no chat box, no panels, no icons in colored circles.

Art style: smooth stylized low-poly, like Dorfromantik and Monument Valley 2. Soft matte materials, gentle global light from the upper left, soft ambient occlusion and short soft shadows, a calm pale sea in desaturated teal-gray with subtle shoreline foam. Muted palette with one warm accent for lit windows and sails. Clean silhouettes, low detail, very readable at small size. Not photorealistic, not busy, no glowing neon, no purple gradient, no hard-hat workers, no smoke.
