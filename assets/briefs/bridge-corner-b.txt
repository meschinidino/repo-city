Bridge corner piece, short arms — Repo City sprite pipeline brief
(second brief; the first asked for 1.2-width arms but both runs drew
long arms with mid posts that cannot trim without dangling rails)

Goal: a SMALL corner deck piece that joins two straight bridge decks
at a sharp bend. The piece owns only the joint: short arms, one post.

References attached: the straight deck tile
(assets/sprites/bridge-a.png) for wood, posts and rope style, and a
close-up screenshot of a bent bridge today showing the fault
(crossing planks, doubled posts, a water gap at the outer edge).

Match the reference art: stylized low-poly game art, warm soft light
from the upper left, matte wood, warm brown planks with visible seams
and nail dots, square posts, rope rails.

Make: ONE SMALL corner piece in screen space, a V (chevron) band
opening to the RIGHT (east), EXACTLY mirror-symmetric top to bottom.
The outer vertex points LEFT; the top arm runs up-right at 26 degrees
above horizontal; the bottom arm mirrors it (interior angle 53
degrees). Deck band width matches the reference tile's deck width at
the same scale. Each arm is SHORT: exactly 4 rung planks long from the
vertex (about 1.2 deck-widths), ending in a clean straight cut across
the arm.

Construction: SHORT planks run ACROSS each arm like rungs of a ladder
(perpendicular to the arm axis, exactly like the reference tile),
fanning around the vertex with no gaps; two nail dots per plank.
Exactly ONE post in the whole piece: a stout square corner post at the
outer vertex point. NO other posts anywhere. One rope rail runs from
the vertex post along the OUTER edge of each arm and ENDS at the arm
cut (a clean cut rope end is correct: the straight decks continue the
rail). The inner edges carry matching rails from a small inner post
just back from the inner point, also ending at the cuts. No water, no
ground, no shadow outside the piece, no text, no people.

Background: solid magenta #FF00FF everywhere outside the piece,
including the V opening between the arms. The WHOLE piece sits inside
the frame with magenta margin on all four sides; nothing touches the
frame edges. One corner piece only, filling most of the frame.
